AirBnb Deep Dive

Airbnb was founded in 2008 with the simple idea of helping people feel like they belong anywhere. Since then, it's evolved into a global platform connecting millions of guests and hosts across more than 220 countries.

The company's culture emphasizes creativity, trust, and the power of community.

When interviewing at Airbnb, expect a strong focus on values alignment, collaboration, and storytelling. Interviewers want to understand how you think, how you solve problems, and how your experiences demonstrate empathy and adaptability.

You should be prepared to:

  • Describe a time you helped build community or foster inclusion.
  • Walk through how you've navigated ambiguity or solved a problem with limited resources.
