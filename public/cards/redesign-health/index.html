The Redesign Health Model

Our approach to company-building is comprehensive. We are deeply involved in the entire process, from idea to scale, delivering a repeatable model that leverages our vast network and centralized resources to accelerate the innovation process and build out an impressive array of solutions, scaling them to improve the health of millions of people.

IDEATEIDEATE

We don’t wait for potential founders to bring good ideas our way. Instead, we build the momentum ourselves, working with our experienced team to concept, research, and validate world-changing ideas.

BUILDBUILD

Once we land on a good idea, we recruit the best founders to the business and throw our considerable weight behind them as they bring the concept to market.

SCALESCALE

We stay close to our companies after launch, giving them the support to grow rapidly and the assets to increase their influence exponentially.

Built at Redesign

We launch companies that create immediate change, because our health simply can’t afford to wait. Since 2018, exceptional founding teams have built over two dozen healthcare businesses at Redesign Health, touching the lives of more than 10 million people across verticals including cancer care, teleaudiology, senior care, COVID-19 testing, and metabolic health.
