One-to-Many Defense

Leonidas high-power microwave answers a swarm the way a swarm attacks: many at once. It is the center of Epirus Point Defense, the system that plans, senses, decides and defeats around the asset that must survive.

One pulse, many drones

In live fire at Camp Atterbury, one instant pulse defeated a 49-drone swarm.

A deep
electronic magazine

It runs on electricity from a standard generator, so it keeps engaging at negligible cost per shot.

Low collateral

Software sets exactly when and where it defeats drones, so operators can hold designated safety zones.

Stops dark drones

Proven effects against fiber-optic-controlled and autonomous UAVs, with reliable performance against a range of evasive behaviors.

One Architecture. Every
Layer Connected.
